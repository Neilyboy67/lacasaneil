About the property

A boutique apartment in the heart of Calahonda

Freshly refurbished for the 2026 holiday season and beyond, our luxury 2-bed apartment blends modern comfort with Spanish charm. Gleaming marble floors throughout, a brand-new designer bathroom, and mesmerising sea views that stretch to the horizon. The south-facing terrace serves up unforgettable sunsets — perfect for romantic evenings, leisurely BBQs and quiet mornings with coffee.

Inside: a fully-equipped kitchen, living area with smart TV and WiFi, and a bright sunroom that doubles as a home office. Two spacious bedrooms (one double, one twin) plus a sofa bed sleep four. Air conditioning in the lounge and master bedroom. Four communal pools just steps from the door, Oxygen Gym on the doorstep, and free parking outside.

About the property

A boutique apartment in the heart of Calahonda

Freshly refurbished for the 2026 holiday season and beyond, our luxury 2-bed apartment blends modern comfort with Spanish charm. Gleaming marble floors throughout, a brand-new designer bathroom, and mesmerising sea views that stretch to the horizon. The south-facing terrace serves up unforgettable sunsets — perfect for romantic evenings, leisurely BBQs and quiet mornings with coffee.

Inside: a fully-equipped kitchen, living area with smart TV and WiFi, and a bright sunroom that doubles as a home office. Two spacious bedrooms (one double, one twin) plus a sofa bed sleep four. Air conditioning in the lounge and master bedroom. Four communal pools just steps from the door, Oxygen Gym on the doorstep, and free parking outside.

Frequently asked

Common questions, answered honestly

How far is La Casa Neil from Málaga Airport?

About 30 km — 30 minutes by car on the AP-7. Pre-booked private transfers cost €45-60 each way for up to 4 guests; taxis €50-70; hire cars work out cheapest if you'll do more than two day trips. Read the full transport guide →

Is parking included?

Yes — free on-street parking directly outside the building, with plenty of space year-round.

Do I need a hire car in Calahonda?

Not strictly. Most amenities — beach, restaurants, supermarkets — are within a 5–15 minute walk. But if you'll do more than two day trips (Marbella, Málaga, Ronda, Mijas Pueblo), a hire car pays for itself by day three.

Is the apartment suitable for families with young children?

Yes. Two bedrooms (one double, one twin), cot available, sofa-bed in the lounge, communal pools with shallow ends, and Calahonda beach (gentle gradient) within an 8-minute walk. Many of our guests come with primary-age children.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes — in the lounge and the master bedroom. The twin bedroom and sunroom have fans. The apartment is also heated for winter stays.

Can I book directly and skip Airbnb fees?

Yes. Booking directly via the enquiry form saves the 14–17% Airbnb service fee. Neil responds personally within an hour to confirm availability and arrange payment by bank transfer or card link.

What's the check-in process?

Self check-in with a key-safe — Neil sends the code on the day of arrival. No queueing at a reception desk. Arrive any time after 4 pm.

Can I stay for a month or longer?

Absolutely — our longest-staying guests come for 4–8 weeks between October and March. Long stays receive preferential pricing. Just mention your dates in the enquiry form and Neil will reply with a tailored quote.
